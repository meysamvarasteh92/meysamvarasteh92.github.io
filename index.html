About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, supervised by Professor Robin Burke. My research broadly focuses on recommender systems, explainable and controllable machine learning, and responsible AI.

I am particularly interested in understanding how recommendation algorithms make decisions and how those decisions affect different stakeholders. A major direction of my research is transparency in recommender systems, especially from the perspective of item and content providers.
